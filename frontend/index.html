Nobody in the hall should miss the talk.

International students, guests and staff sit in the same hall, but a talk is given in one language. An interpreter is not available at every event, and printed summaries arrive after the moment has passed.

B-Translate puts the talk on every phone in the hall, in Turkish or English, while it is happening.

The speaker talks.
The whole room understands.

What you get in the hall

Everything happens in the browser on your phone or laptop.

Read it as it is spoken

Text arrives line by line while the speaker talks, first as a faded draft and then as the final sentence.

Listen instead of reading

Turn on spoken translation and follow with headphones. Volume and mute are always one tap away.

Big screen mode

TV mode shows large subtitles for a projector or a screen beside the stage.

Quiet when the room is quiet

Only real speech goes to translation. Pauses, music and applause do not fill the screen.

A record of every session

Organisers download the full transcript of the day as TXT, CSV or JSON after the event.

How it works

From the speaker’s microphone to your screen in three steps.

  1. 1

    Scan the code

    Scan the QR code shown in the hall, or open this page and tap the join button.

  2. 2

    Follow the talk

    The host speaks. Each sentence is recognised, translated with DeepL and sent to your screen.

  3. 3

    Read or listen

    Pick your interface language, turn on audio or switch to TV mode whenever you like.

Questions people ask

Do I need to install an app or create an account?

No. The live page runs in any current mobile or desktop browser. Scan the code or open the link and you are in.

Which languages are supported?

Turkish to English and English to Turkish. The host chooses the direction and can switch it during the talk.

How quickly does the translation appear?

A draft appears while the sentence is still being spoken and is replaced by the final translation a moment after the speaker finishes it.

How accurate is it?

Speech is recognised automatically and translated by DeepL, so names, technical terms and fast or unclear speech can come out wrong. Use it as a live aid, not as an official transcript.

Can I listen instead of reading?

Yes. Turn on audio on the live page and use headphones so you do not disturb the people around you.

Does the page use my microphone?

No. As a participant your device only receives the broadcast. Only the host’s computer captures sound.

Why is there no join button right now?

The button only appears while a host is broadcasting. The page checks regularly, so keep it open and the button appears when the session starts.
